## Dead end (no real buyer volume, don't chase these as SEO targets)
- "engineering pod" / "dedicated engineering pod" / "dedicated development pod" — Google reads "pod" as Kubernetes pods or hardware/brand names (e.g. "Henry Engineering talent pod"), not a staffing concept. Near-zero genuine search volume across every variant tested.
- "remote engineering team" / "hire remote engineers" — heavily contaminated with job-seeker queries ("software engineer jobs", "remote engineer jobs"), not buyer queries. Low usable signal.
- Keep "Engineering Pod" as the branded product name in on-page copy (it already is, on `/for/startups/`), but don't expect it to carry organic search weight on its own.
